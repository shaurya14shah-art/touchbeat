Touchbeat / Lab

How it works, and how far to trust it

The pipeline

Everything runs in your browser. MediaPipe finds 33 body points and a general object detector finds the ball. A touch is the moment your toe is closest to the top of the ball, timed against the beat with a short warm-up to absorb your camera's delay.

The first version used the highest point of the foot. Checking frame by frame showed that fires about 150ms before contact, because the foot lifts and then lands, which is why the detector measures contact instead.

Measured touch detection

Scored against touches labelled from individual video frames by an AI assistant (Claude), using the same code the game runs.

What it learned from coaching videos

Posture limits, the form score and the cyan ghost come from skeletons extracted from the videos below. The videos themselves are never published, only anonymous averaged motion. The form score compares your posture with what those players did. It measures similarity to them, not correctness, and is not medical or professional coaching advice.
